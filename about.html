WHAT THIS IS ABOUT

SpaceTime With Robert is a public telescope outreach project focused on sharing astronomy directly with the community.

At outreach events, people are encouraged to look through the telescope and sometimes even sign it afterward.

Once a telescope becomes fully funded through donations, or naturally retires after years of outreach, it is cleaned, refurbished, and donated to local schools for student use.

Donations help cover outreach costs, repairs, transportation, astronomy equipment, and future telescope donations.

Since 2017, multiple telescopes have been donated to public schools, a private school, a community college, and a local Girl Scout troop.

HOW IT STARTED

This outreach project began in 2017 with a simple idea: bring telescopes outside and let people use them.

Early support included roughly $600 in donations, which forced an important question: how should outreach support be responsibly reinvested?

The answer became a cycle: observe, share, fund, donate, repeat.

TELESCOPE OUTREACH ERAS

Diamond Era · 2016

Before official outreach began, a small 114mm Dobsonian named Diamond was used for training and learning telescope operation.

This period lasted roughly six months and established the foundation for future public outreach.

John I Era · 2017–2018

The first official outreach telescope: a 10" Dobsonian named John I.

This telescope became heavily signed during outreach events before eventually being retired and donated to a local school in 2018.

John II Era

Outreach expanded significantly during this era, continuing the telescope donation cycle and increasing public observing events.

The Isaac Era

Spring 2023: Acquired a new Explore Scientific Firstlight 10” Dobsonian named Isaac.

Isaac became the most successful outreach telescope so far, eventually reaching thousands of visitors.
